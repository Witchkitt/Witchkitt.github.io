The Cauldron

The Cauldron grew out of firsthand experience as an independent artist, and the frustration of seeing how difficult it can be for good music to reach real listeners.

Independent artists can spend years writing, recording, releasing, and promoting music and still struggle to break through the noise.

The Cauldron gives artists a place to submit music, lets listeners vote, and turns standout songs into recognition through charts, playlists, editorial features, and other discovery opportunities.

It is built around recognition and discovery, not pay-to-play.
